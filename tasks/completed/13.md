markers.step: `markers.next-marker` into a local m, then a flat sequence of `m <byte> i32.eq [ ... ] when` branches (each leaves the stack unchanged; refusing branches end in `trap`). Dispatch:
- 0xD8 (second SOI): NOT_JPEG. 0xD9 (EOI before any scan): NO_FRAME "EOI with no scan". 0xD0 to 0xD7 (RSTn outside entropy data): BAD_RESTART. 0x01 (TEM): UNSUPPORTED_SOF "reserved marker".
- 0xC0 0xC1 0xC2 0xC9 0xCA: `markers.segment` then `markers.sof` with m.
- 0xC3 0xC5 0xC6 0xC7 0xCB 0xCD 0xCE 0xCF (lossless, hierarchical, differential), 0xC8, 0xDE (DHP), 0xDF (EXP), 0xF0 to 0xFD (JPGn): UNSUPPORTED_SOF with the marker byte in the message (refuse before reading a length). The corpus has 14 lossless SOF3 files (corpus/wild/lossless/*, corpus/synthetic/lossless*-psv1.jpg) that must come out UNSUPPORTED_SOF.
- 0xC4 DHT, 0xDB DQT, 0xDD DRI: segment then the parser. 0xCC DAC: segment, skipped in M0 (parsed in M5; comment). 0xE0: segment then markers.app0. 0xEE: segment then markers.app14. Other 0xE1 to 0xEF and 0xFE (COM): segment, skipped by length (contents never copied anywhere).
- 0xDA: segment then markers.sos; the walk then stops because a scan is set.
- Anything else (including 0xDC DNL before a scan and the reserved 0x02 to 0xBF): UNSUPPORTED_SOF "unexpected marker".
