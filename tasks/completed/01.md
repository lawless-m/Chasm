Correction (2026-10-09): the description's premise that jpegtran's lossless transform decodes to exactly the turned pixels holds only for orientations 1 to 4 (greyscale, or colour with -nosmooth). For 5 to 8 libjpeg's integer IDCT rounds its row and column passes differently, and fancy upsampling's rounding bias depends on position, so the decodes differ by 1 or 2 (measured: greyscale max 1, colour max 2; a wrong mapping differs by far more). The verify now requires byte equality where it holds and, for every orientation and both upsampling modes, the same header and a largest difference of at most 2. orient.py's docstring says the same.
