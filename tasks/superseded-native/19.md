Repo: /home/matt/Git/Whackford. Read `crates/runtime/src/proc.rs` (`prog_open/read/write/close`, `kill`) and `crates/runtime/src/native.rs` (the async `ring_enter`). M11 decision: killing a process is a write of `kill` to `/prog/<pid>/ctl`; reading `/prog` lists live pids as directory records; handled by the scheduler before the namespace, in the runner's `ring_enter` for OPEN paths starting with `/prog` and for READ/WRITE/CLOSE on handles the scheduler issued (keep the scheduler's handles distinct from `NativeHost`'s by routing on a handle set the scheduler owns). A killed parked process never resumes: its pending park is resolved with a cancellation that makes its host future return an error the driver treats as a quiet exit (not a trap; `scheduler.exit`); a running process that kills itself exits at that write; a killed process's `wack.spawn` closure root is unrooted. Tests in `crates/runtime/tests/procs.rs`: a program that spawns a receiver parked on a channel, writes `kill` to its ctl with `write-file` (the pid is 1), closes the channel, and finishes without the receiver printing; `ls "/prog"`-style listing printed by a program shows `0/` and `1/`; writing `stop` returns -6 and `/prog/7/ctl` opens as -1.
