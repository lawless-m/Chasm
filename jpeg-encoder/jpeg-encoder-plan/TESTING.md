# Testing

## The oracle

`../../jpeg-decoder/oracle/libjpeg-turbo-3.2.0/bin/cjpeg`, the same build
as the decoder's `djpeg` (built `-DWITH_SIMD=0`; see the decoder corpus's
`SOURCES.md`). Every encoder comparison runs it as

```
cjpeg -dct int -quality Q -sample S [-grayscale for P5] [-icc PROFILE] -outfile ref.jpg in.ppm
```

with `-sample 2x2`, `2x1` or `1x1` for `420`, `422`, `444`. The gate is
byte equality of the two files.

## Unit tests

Each word gets `test` lines from the standard or from cjpeg's own values:
the quality-scaled tables at q 1, 50, 85, 100; known DCT input and output
blocks; Huffman codes for sample DC and AC values; stuffing and final-byte
padding; colour conversion at the corners of the cube; the eight
orientations on a 2x3 image; the EXIF and ICC parsers on hand-built
segments, valid and each refusal.

## The harness

`tools/harness.py` assembles `encode` or `clean` from its file list and
runs it under the decoder harness's CPU and memory caps, with `--jobs`.
Classes per case: `match`, `mismatch`, `refused CODE`, `trap`, `hang`,
`cpu`, `memory`. A mismatch keeps both files and reports the first
differing byte offset and which marker or MCU it falls in.

Modes:

- `encode`: PPM inputs over the grid, against cjpeg.
- `clean`: JPEG inputs; the reference is
  `cjpeg [-icc p] (rotate(djpeg -dct int x))`, where `rotate` is
  `tools/orient.py` (a plain-Python reference for the eight orientations
  on PPM) and `p` is the profile `clean` should carry (taken from the input
  by Python, checked by the rules in SECURITY.md). A file djpeg refuses or
  our decoder refuses is classed as the decoder harness classes it.
- `roundtrip`: every encoder output is also decoded by our decoder and by
  djpeg, and the two decodes must match.

## The grid

- Quality: 1, 10, 40, 50, 75, 85, 90, 95, 100.
- Sampling: 420, 422, 444; greyscale (P5) once per quality.
- Sizes, generated: 1x1, 7x9, 8x8, 16x16, 17x33, 1x1000, 1000x1, and one
  large (about 4000x3000).

## Inputs

- The decoder corpus's decodable files, decoded by the oracle's djpeg to
  PPM (8-bit and the `p12-*` 12-bit ones): `encode` inputs; the JPEGs
  themselves: `clean` inputs.
- `jpeg-encoder/corpus/synthetic/`: generated by `tools/gen_meta.py` from
  `testorig.ppm`: each orientation 1 to 8; broken EXIF (bad byte order,
  IFD0 offset or count past the segment, wrong type or count for 0x0112,
  orientation 0 and 9); ICC cases (single and multi-chunk, a chunk missing,
  duplicated, out of order, count mismatch, size field wrong, no `acsp`,
  a tag past the end, over 1 MB, a CMYK profile on a CMYK input, a GRAY
  profile on an RGB image).
- The real-world set: a few hundred files from the Brandbank T0 share
  copied into `tmp/` (git-ignored; company product images, never
  committed). A full pass over the share's 16,224 files once the cores are
  free.
- E7: the decoder's mutation fuzzer pointed at `clean`.

## CI

As the decoder: `wack fmt --check`, `wack test` on the encoder files, and
the Whackford repository's "Before pushing" list. During the M8 fuzz week,
builds and tests run with at most 8 jobs.
